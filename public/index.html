How it works

From first message to first PR

The same four steps whether you train in person or online.

  1. Free consultation

    Send a message. We talk through your goals, training history, injuries and schedule, and you get pricing.

  2. Assessment

    Baseline strength, movement quality and starting measurements, so progress is measured and not guessed.

  3. Your program

    A plan written for you: exercises, sets, reps and loads, matched to your equipment and the days you have.

  4. Check-ins

    Regular reviews of your numbers. Weights go up, the plan adapts, and you always know what's next.

FAQ

Before you message

How much does training cost?

Pricing depends on the format and how often you train. Send a message through the form below and you'll get current rates along with your free consultation.

Do I need gym experience?

No. Programs start from where you are today. Beginners get more coaching on technique; experienced lifters get more on programming and progression.

How does online coaching work?

You receive a custom program to run at your own gym, then check in on a set schedule. Your plan is adjusted based on your results and feedback.

Where do in-person sessions take place?

In-person training covers Marietta, East Cobb and the Greater Atlanta area. The exact location is confirmed during your consultation.
