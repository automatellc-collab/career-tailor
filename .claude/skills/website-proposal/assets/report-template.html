Findings

What we found on your current site

Each issue, and what it means for getting calls.

Third-party code slows every visit

Google Tag Manager (456 KB) and Facebook tracking (208 KB) freeze the page for about 1.1 seconds. A YouTube video downloads 1.1 MB before anyone presses play.

Why it mattersVisitors on phones wait on code they never see. The video can load only when someone taps it, and tracking can be trimmed to what you actually use.

Outdoor lighting isn't on the home page

Your yard signs lead with “Afraid of the dark? Outdoor light installation.” The home page lists six services, and lighting isn't one of them.

Why it mattersYour signs bring people to your website, and the lighting service they came for isn't there. The redesign gives lighting its own section with a day/night comparison.

The Google search snippet is jumbled

The description Google shows is the full home page text in one run-on line, including a raw YouTube link. Your SEO plugin generated it automatically.

Why it mattersThat snippet is your first impression in search results. A clear, written description gets more clicks.

No local business information for Google

The site doesn't use structured “local business” data: business type, phone, city, service area and services.

Why it mattersThat data helps Google show you for searches like “landscaper near me.” The redesign includes it.

Hard to tap on a phone

Some buttons and links are too small or too close together. Seven links have vague wording like “read more.” Only one link on the page dials your number.

Why it mattersEvery missed tap is a lost call. The redesign keeps a Call button on screen as visitors scroll.

Repeat visits aren't faster

87 files are set up so phones don't keep them between visits.

Why it mattersPeople often look at a contractor's site several times before calling. Each visit should be quicker than the last.
